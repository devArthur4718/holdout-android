One counter. That's the whole idea.

Your last cigarette, drink or bet starts the clock. Holdout shows how long you've held out, the money you didn't spend and the time you got back, for smoking, alcohol, sugar, vaping, social media, gambling or any habit you name.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no server, no analytics, no ads. Holdout doesn't even ask for internet access, so it can't send your data anywhere. Everything stays on your phone.

A SLIP IS NOT A FAILURE
Logging a slip restarts the counter, but your longest streak and all the money and time you've saved are kept. The wording never shames.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Today: a live counter inside an arc that fills up to your next goal, with money kept, time back and your personal best
• Trophies at 1 day, 1 week, 30, 60 and 90 days, 6 months, 1 year and beyond, plus personal goals you set yourself ("45 days", "Beach trip")
• Stats: money kept and time back across every streak, with a six-month savings chart
• Calendar: every day since you started, clean or slip
• Reasons: why you're quitting, in your own words
• Optional trophy notifications, never between 22:00 and 08:00
• Several habits at once
• Export everything to CSV, or delete all your data in one tap
• English, Português (Brasil) and Español

Holdout is a self-tracking tool, not a medical service. If you need help to quit, talk to a health professional or a local support service.
