# Holdout — Privacy Policy

_Effective 26 November 2026_

Holdout ("the app") is made by Arthur Gomes da Silva. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter in the app. The short version: **it stays on your phone, and we never receive it.**

## What the app stores

To work, Holdout keeps the following on your device, in the app's private storage:

- the habits you track: name, type, start time, and the optional cost and time per day you enter;
- the slips you log, with the optional trigger you pick;
- the reasons you write down for quitting;
- the personal goals you add;
- your settings, such as whether trophy notifications are on.

## What the app does not do

- **No collection, no sharing.** The app does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device. We have no server and receive none of your data.
- **No account.** You never sign up or sign in.
- **No ads, analytics or tracking.** The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting libraries.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** (Android 13+, only if you turn on trophy notifications): to tell you when you reach a goal. Notifications are created on the device.
- **Run at startup**: to set trophy reminders again after the phone restarts. It reads nothing.

## Backups

Android can include the app's data in your phone's own backup (for example Google's device backup), so you keep your progress on a new phone. Android and your backup provider handle that backup under their own terms; you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

## Export and deletion

- **Export:** Settings › Export as CSV saves all your data to a file at a location you choose. What happens to that file afterwards is up to you.
- **Delete:** Settings › Delete all data erases everything in the app. Uninstalling the app also removes it.

## Children

Holdout is not directed at children under 13 and is meant for adults.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
